Idea 01 · ClipHarvest

Turn one long webinar into short, captioned clips.

Who pays?
Coaches, consultants, and course creators hosting recurring webinars.
The problem
Useful long-form recordings often sit unused because editing clips takes too much time.
Core MVP
Upload a recording, identify clips, generate captions, and deliver ready-to-post files.
Validation test
Offer to repurpose one recent webinar and watch whether creators ask for the next batch.

Idea 44 · RenewAlert

Track vendor renewals before auto-billing surprises arrive.

Who pays?
Small businesses juggling a dozen or more vendor subscriptions and contracts.
The problem
Renewal windows and price increases are scattered across emails, dashboards, and forgotten contracts.
Core MVP
Vendor database, renewal dates, price notes, and scheduled alerts before action windows close.
Money note
The pitch is easiest when one avoided unwanted renewal can pay for the tool.
